About BLA³Q

We are the students and teachers of BLA³Q Student Project, an organization created at Bedford Academy with the goal of expanding (inter)national initiative to inspire students to be self-sufficient and create meaningful change. Our mission is to foster collaborative learning, self-suffiency, innovative thinking, and revolutionary, real-world problem-solving skills among students and educators.

To create a global network of educational institutions and students working together to address contemporary challenges through education, technology, creativity, cooperatives, skill-sharing, and interdisciplinary collaboration.

Current Initiative: The MET Trip

We are on a quest to send fifty (50) of our fellow students from the African-American Studies and Advanced Placement Seminar classes to the Metropolitan Museum of Art on Friday, October 24, 2025, for the Superfine 'Black' Dandyism exhibition.

All students will receive a copy of the required reading: Slaves to Fashion - Black Dandyism and the Styling of Black Diasporic Identity by Monica Miller, curator of the Superfine exhibit.
